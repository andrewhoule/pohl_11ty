<p class="intro">Hello everyone! My name is Grace Houle, I am married to a wonderful husband named <a href="http://andyhoule.com">Andrew Houle</a>. We have three kids Jayden (2), Isabella (4), Timothy (5), another on the way, and one baby that is waiting for us in Heaven (miscarriage).</p>
<p>Our oldest son just started Kindergarten. Yikes. My kids are my joy, and I am blessed EVERY single day by being a part of their lives. I was raised in a pretty big family, there were 7 kids, and 3 more that went to be with the Lord early (miscarriages) I have lived in the country most of my life, and LOVE it. I moved to a more suburban area for five years, and missed my country setting, but now I am back so all is well.</p>
<h2>Why I started blogging?</h2>
<p>About 3 years ago my husband was running a successful blog called MyInkBlog (he has since sold it) about all things web related, and one day randomly in conversation he suggested to me about writing a blog. I brushed it off, because I really couldn’t think of a topic that I would want to passionately write about on a consistent basis, but God saw the big picture and was already planting the seeds of future events.</p>
<p>Well, during that season of life I was really praying about what God wanted me to do, I had tried different ministry opportunities but I wanted to minister in a place that I could still be with my kids. Than the thought came to mind to blog about Christian topics. The hopes would be to seek out what I truly believed, and share my conclusions with others. Along my journey, I have also added family testimonials, because it is such a huge part of my life and growing experiences.</p>

<h2>Purpose for blogging</h2>
<p>My passion is to encourage other woman and see their life transformed by Christ. I have always desired a community website, where I hear from my readers on what’s going on in their life and their personal story, it truly blesses my heart and increases my faith. I know lives are not transformed unless God is a part of the process, so when I witness or hear testimonies of lives that are changed I know it is a miracle, and I am thrilled to be a part of it.</p>
