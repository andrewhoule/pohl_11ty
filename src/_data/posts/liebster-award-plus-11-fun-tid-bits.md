<p class="intro">I am excited to announce that Poured Out His Love won the Liebster Award! It&#8217;s an award given to blogs with less than 200 followers in any one social outlet. It&#8217;s designed to show recognition and appreciation for an up-and-coming blog. Poured Out His Love was nominated by <a href="http://living-story.weebly.com">Living Story</a>, a great blog that you should definitely check out; you&#8217;re sure to receive an extra dose of encouragement this morning!</p>
<h2>11 Fun Tid Bits About Poured Out His Love</h2>
<p>Here is a little inside scoop about my blog based on 11 questions from Living Story.</p>
<h3>1. Why do you blog?</h3>
<p>I blog to encourage other women&#8217;s faith in God, and to live this life with purpose; while realizing even in the every day challenges God has a blueprint for us in His word that will help us live victorious lives.</p>
<h3>2. How did you come up with a name for your blog?</h3>
<p>My husband and I were just rattling off some blog name ideas, when he suggested pouredoutHislove.com I knew it was perfect.</p>
<h3>3. Can you tell me a little about your family?</h3>
<p>I am married to my best bud <a href="http://www.andyhoule.com">Andrew Houle</a>, who is a web developer. I have three amazing kids Timothy, Isabella and Jayden. I love spending time with them! My oldest son Timothy loves board games, school, and art. My daughter Isabella loves singing, animals, and hanging out with friends and family. And Jayden my youngest loves anything related to sports, and those tube things the kids crawl through.</p>
<h3>4. How often do you blog?</h3>
<p>I blog once per week. I might start getting in a more frequent routine, but right now that is all the time I can muster!</p>
<h3>5. What goals do you have for your writing/blog?</h3>
<p>My goal is to use blogging as a platform to perhaps write a devotional ebook/book. My daily goals are to share as much truth as I know to as many people I can, and see lives transformed by God’s goodness.</p>
<h3>6. What do you like most about blogging?</h3>
<p>Being able to invest and encourage others even though I may not even know them.</p>
<h3>7. What do you like least about blogging?</h3>
<p>Putting yourself on a vulnerable platform. It is easy for people to scrutinize you when you publish things for everyone to read.</p>
<h3>8. What causes are you passionate about? Share them with me!</h3>
<p>I love supporting anything or anyone that is aligning themselves with God’s standards as viewed in the Bible.</p>
<h3>9. If you could only let people know one thing about you, what would it be?</h3>
<p>This is such a hard question for me!! I guess it would be that I have always considered myself comfortably ordinary. I never really felt I excelled at anything beyond my peers and yet God chose to use me for His glory. God looks for obedient hearts, not necessarily the lead in the play.</p>
<h3>10. What is your favorite type of music and why?</h3>
<p>Contemporary Christian. It always encourages me, no matter what mood I am in. Some particular artists are Casting Crowns, Matt Maher, Sarah Groves, Matt Redman</p>
<h3>11. Which of your posts are you most proud of?  Be sure to link it!</h3>
<p>I am most proud of my post about my son <a href="/why-i-am-not-looking-forward-to-my-son-going-kindergarten">Timothy going to kindergarten</a>. Not necessarily because I wrote it perfectly, but because I got to reflect on how proud I am of my son, and that is an awesome feeling as a mom.</p>
