<p><strong><em>Dear Son,</em></strong></p>
<p>Don&#8217;t get me wrong; it&#8217;s not that I am not excited for you. I am. I know you will excel in school and do awesome. <strong>You are super smart;</strong> you always have been. You recognized the alphabet at 2 years old. That seems advanced to me.</p>
<p><strong>You love to learn.</strong> Your teacher already picked that up at kindergarten evaluation. They flattered me with the notion that they almost wanted to advance you to first grade. Your brightness shows.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-3917" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-baby-300x400.jpg" alt="Tim baby" width="300" height="400" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-baby-300x400.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-baby-562x750.jpg 562w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px" /></p>
<p>I am not worried that you will be wrongly influenced by those around you. (Although I will still always pray for you) <strong>You have always stood up for truth;</strong> it didn&#8217;t matter what age was doing wrong.</p>
<p><strong>I am not worried that you won&#8217;t make friends</strong>. Even the toughest critics will be softened by your jokes.</p>
<blockquote><p>Tim: There is a maple tree in the ground&#8230;Joke. (Yes, he emphatically ends all his jokes with the verbal word &#8220;joke&#8221;, and yes this is one of his &#8220;jokes&#8221;)</p></blockquote>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-3922" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/tim-1--400x300.jpg" alt="tim 1" width="400" height="300" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/tim-1--400x300.jpg 400w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/tim-1--750x562.jpg 750w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/tim-1-.jpg 1000w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 400px) 100vw, 400px" /></p>
<p><strong>No, the transition will come easy for you</strong>. You like structure, are super smart and feed off of positive accountability.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-3926" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-3-400x400.jpg" alt="Tim 3" width="400" height="400" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-3-400x400.jpg 400w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-3-150x150.jpg 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-3.jpg 612w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 400px) 100vw, 400px" /></p>
<h2><em>But it&#8217;s still hard for Mom.</em></h2>
<p>It&#8217;s hard leaving you in someone else&#8217;s care for 6 plus hours a day (plus bus time).</p>
<p>It&#8217;s hard to think of missing your smiling face even though you are in good hands.</p>
<p>It&#8217;s hard to conceptualize how quick 5 years went and how much quicker the next 15 will go.</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-3927" src="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-4-400x265.jpg" alt="Tim 4" width="400" height="265" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-4-400x265.jpg 400w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-4-750x498.jpg 750w, /wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Tim-4.jpg 1000w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 400px) 100vw, 400px" /></p>
<p>You are my bud. The oldest child of the three musketeers. Always the first volunteer for prayer with a contagious laugh that has a domino effect to those around you. Artistic and funny with a perseverance that helps you excel at anything you put your mind to.</p>
<p>Mom and Dad are so proud of you.</p>
<p>Always know wherever you are, you are missed, you are loved, and Mom always wants a hug!</p>
