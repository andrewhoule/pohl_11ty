<p>Because most toddler&#8217;s attention span is all of a nanosecond, I find the simpler the craft the better. Age and personality type also play a factor when finding applicable crafts for your sweethearts.</p>
<p>My children have two very different personalities. Even by age 2, my son could stay on a task for quite a while, whereas my daughter is just like me, “blessed”, with a very small attention span. We both start a hundred things at once and don&#8217;t finish them.</p>
<h2>Mark 4:35-40</h2>
<p>This craft is simple, yet fun. It serves to reinforce the Bible story of Jesus calming the sea. It&#8217;s a nice reminder that when the world is raging around us, God is in control of it all. He can calm the storms in our life.</p>

<h2>What You&#8217;ll Need</h2>
<ul>
<li>Construction Paper</li>
<li>Tape</li>
<li>Popsicle Sticks</li>
<li>Glue</li>
<li>Scissors</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to Create Your Craft</h2>
<ol>
<li>Cut out a half moon and a triangle.
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3005" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-edited.jpg" alt="photo-edited" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-edited-400x300.jpg 400w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-edited-750x562.jpg 750w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
<li>Glue one popsicle stick in the center of the triangle.
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3008" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-1-edited.jpg" alt="photo-(1)-edited" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-1-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-1-edited-400x300.jpg 400w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-1-edited-750x562.jpg 750w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
<li>Glue half moon on the top side of the base of the same popsicle stick in step 2.
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3013" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-7-edited.jpg" alt="photo-(7)-edited" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-7-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-7-edited-400x300.jpg 400w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-7-edited-750x562.jpg 750w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
<li>On reverse side of half moon, glue popsicle stick on base of previous popsicle stick. Put boat aside.
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3010" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-2-edited.jpg" alt="photo-(2)-edited" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-2-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-2-edited-400x300.jpg 400w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-2-edited-750x562.jpg 750w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
<li>Cut a strip of construction paper that looks like water; glue it to the base of another piece of construction paper.
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3014" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-6-edited.jpg" alt="photo-(6)-edited" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-6-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-6-edited-400x300.jpg 400w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-6-edited-750x562.jpg 750w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
<li>Cut a second strip of water and place it on top of previous water, bend farthest edges inward and place a piece of tape in the inner fold. This will create a pocket. Make sure not to make the waves too big, as it can cause the boat to snag.
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3015" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-3-edited.jpg" alt="photo-(3)-edited" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-3-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-3-edited-400x300.jpg 400w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-3-edited-750x562.jpg 750w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
<li>Grab your boat and place it in the pocket, you can travel it back and forth in the water.</li>
<li>Add &#8220;Jesus Calms the Sea&#8221; at the top of the page, and have the kids color it if you like. Voila, the craft is complete!
<p><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="size-full wp-image-2997" src="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-5-edited.jpg" alt="Sea Craft Finished. Complete with a caption written by my 3 year old :)" width="1200" height="900" srcset="/assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-5-edited.jpg 1200w, /assets/uploads/2013/04/photo-5-edited-400x300.jpg 400w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 1200px) 100vw, 1200px" /></li>
</ol>
