<p>I loved this book! Author Angie Smith was so genuine as she addressed a common problem...fear. Incorporating personal stories and humor to openly discuss this restrictive behavior. Delving into various topics from the fear of rejection to the deep theological matters like the fear of God. There is only one line I didn't agree with which was her statement on doubt. She believes there can be room for doubt as a Christian. Yet I feel that just like Jesus asked Thomas in John 20:27 to stop doubting and believe, we are called to the same standard. However, besides that one difference it was an excellent book and I  would suggest it to anyone. </p>
