<p>Rick Warren does a great job laying down the foundational Biblical truths of Gods Word. This book is great to use for discipleship or just to refresh on why you believe what you believe, and to reinforce it with scripture. Every chapter starts with a quote and scripture, and ends with a point to ponder, verse to remember and question to consider. It either solidifies what you believe, or helps you grow in understanding. Sometimes he repeats certain topics. But I would still give this book a thumbs up.</p>
