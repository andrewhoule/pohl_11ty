<p>I thought this was a great book! In the past I have been a skeptic of others testimonials of Heaven. Yet, John Piper portrays his experience with such genuineness and conviction it encourages one to listen. There are verses throughout the book that reinforce his testimony. His accounts of Heaven are brief. The story mainly focuses on his recovery after knowing he was inches away from eternity in Heaven, and coming to grips that his purpose here on earth wasn't completed.</p>
